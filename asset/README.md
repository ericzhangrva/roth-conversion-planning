# Executive Financial Asset Portfolio Dashboard

This directory contains your interactive financial asset dashboard and balance sheet prepared for your consultation with your financial advisor.

---

## Final Product Overview

* [`index.html`](file:///Users/eric/Dropbox/ai/asset/index.html): **The primary dashboard.** Open directly in Safari or Chrome. Features:
  * **Dynamic Data**: Populated automatically via the JSON ledger.
  * **Executive KPI Summary**: High-level metrics for Retirement Accounts, Real Estate Equity, Cash Reserves, Taxable Brokerage, and Personal Property.
  * **All numbers rounded strictly to $K** for high-level executive reading.
  * **3 Native Vector Donut Charts (Pure SVG) with Interactive JS Tooltips**: 
    1. *Total Net Worth Allocation Donut* ($6,621K)
    2. *Investment Sector Exposure Donut* (6 sectors, $5,102K investable)
    3. *Tax Bucket & Asset Location Profile* ($4,720K pre-tax / 71%)
  * **Comprehensive Balance Sheet**: Clean breakdown by account category and tax status.
  * **Strategic Consultation Agenda**: Tailored talking points on early retirement, tax diversification, college funding, and estate planning.
  * Dedicated **"Export to JPG"** and **"Export to PDF"** buttons located in the top right header for convenient reporting.
