# LinkedIn Strategy Guide: AI for Non-Engineers Series

This guide and folder contains a complete, ready-to-publish LinkedIn content strategy based on your recent journey using AI across five diverse projects:
1. **The Nostalgic Game:** *Bulls & Cows* deployed in 90 minutes ([Play live](https://ericzhangrva.github.io/number-guess/)).
2. **Quantitative Wealth Planning:** 10,000-run Monte Carlo 40-year stochastic tax & asset optimizer.
3. **Personal Health Advocacy:** Longitudinal lab analytics & interactive dashboard for your wife's health.
4. **Deep Theoretical Mathematics:** Distilling Fermat's Last Theorem & the landmark Lean 4 machine verification.
5. **Everyday FinTech App:** *OptiRewards*—smart point-of-sale card optimizer (168 cards across 13 issuers, GPS nearby store detection, point valuation sliders).
6. **The Unfiltered Reality (The Thoughts & Learnings):** The actual gaps, traps, and hard lessons of building with AI as a non-engineer (saved exclusively for the final post).

---

## 1. Structure of the Series

Per your strategy, **thoughts and meta-reflections on AI are held back until the final post**. 
* **Posts 1–6:** Focus purely on the story, what was built, the problem it solved, and tangible demos.
* **Post 7 (The Grand Finale):** Delivers the deep philosophical thoughts, lessons learned, and the honest gaps.
