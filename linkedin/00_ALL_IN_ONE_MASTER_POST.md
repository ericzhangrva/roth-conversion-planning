### 4. The Grand Challenge: Fermat’s Last Theorem
I’ve always been fascinated by pure mathematics. For 358 years, mathematicians tried and failed to prove Fermat's Last Theorem ($a^n + b^n \neq c^n$ for $n \ge 3$) by directly factoring the equation.

With AI as a research partner, I wanted to understand the modern breakthrough:
* We traced how Gerhard Frey turned an arithmetic problem into an elliptic curve ($y^2 = x(x-a^p)(x+b^p)$).
* Traced Ken Ribet's level-lowering and Andrew Wiles’ monumental $R = T$ Modularity Theorem.
* Synthesized the entire 130-page proof into a 7-page, self-contained visual and logical roadmap showing why geometry forbids a solution from existing.
* Documented the historic milestone where multi-agent AI verified this entire proof down to the bedrock axioms in Lean 4 (~13M lines of code).

### 5. Everyday Utility: OptiRewards (Point-of-Sale Card Optimizer)
Most people carry 3 to 7 credit cards, each with different multipliers, rotating 5% quarters, and point valuations. At the checkout counter, you have about two seconds to decide which card to swipe before the cashier looks at you awkwardly.

Almost everyone guesses—and leaves 2% to 4% of cash back on the table.

In a single day, we built **OptiRewards**:
* Indexes **168 credit cards across 13 major banks** (Chase, Amex, Capital One, BofA, Citi, etc.).
* Uses real GPS location to detect nearby businesses within 500 meters (supermarkets, restaurants, gas stations).
* Includes dynamic valuation sliders for points & miles (e.g. valuing Chase UR at 1.8¢ vs. Amex MR at 1.7¢).
* Recommends the highest-yielding card in your wallet in **under one second**.
* Designed with an Apple-first architecture (private Apple CloudKit storage + free global edge CDN) requiring zero server maintenance.

### The Unfiltered Truth: What Are the Real Gaps?
The narrative around AI often swings between "it's magic" and "it's all hype." 

Here is my takeaway as a non-engineer:
1. **AI removes the syntax barrier, not the thinking barrier.** If you don't understand the underlying logic of a Monte Carlo model or an elliptic curve, AI will generate plausible-sounding nonsense with absolute confidence.
2. **Context rot is real.** As projects grow, you must act deliberately. You can’t just say "make it better"—you must structure requirements, maintain strict documentation, and inspect intermediate outputs.
3. **Pacing matters.** The biggest mistake is moving too fast. Asking for explanations and stress-testing edge cases before writing code is what separates working software from broken spaghetti.
4. **Problem definition is 99% of the work.** Typing code is 1% of the effort. Defining exact schemas, edge cases, and requirements is what determines success.

We are living in an era where curiosity is no longer bottlenecked by technical execution.
