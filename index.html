Assumptions
  • All tax calculations explicitly assume a Married Filing Jointly (MFJ) filing status.
  • Roth conversions become accessible 5 years after conversion (or immediately upon reaching age 59½).
  • Optimization limits conversions to keep YE Cash + YE Inv + Accessible Roth ≥ Min Liquidity Buffer.
  • Simulation strictly accounts for SECURE 2.0 RMDs (Age 75+), NIIT, and Medicare IRMAA surcharges.
  • Capital gains taxes on taxable brokerage drawdowns are ignored (assumed 0% for simplicity).
  • Social Security benefits are dynamically scaled based on the standard IRS actuarial curve (relative to the base input).
  • Pre-retirement healthcare is assumed to equal the subsidized cost (due to employer coverage). In Retirement (pre-Medicare), costs remain subsidized unless MAGI exceeds the cliff.
  • Federal tax brackets and standard deductions are inflated annually by the Inflation Rate.
  • College expenses follow a 5-year distribution of the annual input: 50% / 100% / 100% / 100% / 50%.
